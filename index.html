The village Chinese

Hot from the wok, ready to take home

Chalfont Oriental is Little Chalfont's Chinese takeaway, on White Lion Road. You'll know it by the gold sign. Every dish is cooked to order: crispy aromatic duck with pancakes, Hong Kong style sweet & sour, salt & chilli king prawns, and chow mein and fried rice from a hot wok.

Ring ahead and your order will be packed and ready when you arrive.
